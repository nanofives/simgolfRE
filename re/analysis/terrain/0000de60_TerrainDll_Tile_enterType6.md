## Purpose
Raises by 20.0 every mesh vertex that a tile entering terrain type 6 shares with a type-6 neighbour or diagonal, which is what makes a block of type-6 tiles stand up as one raised surface instead of a set of separate steps. The tile's own centre-bottom vertex is always raised (index [this+0x50], which is face 0 slot 2; fld 0x1000de8c, fadd dword [0x1005f51c] = 20.0f at 0x1000de92, fstp 0x1000dea1). The rest is a walk over the four neighbours at [this+0x34] (n0), [this+0x38] (n1), [this+0x3c] (n2) and [this+0x40] (n3), each guarded by the pointer AND by Tile::getType == 6 (je 0x1000deae and jne 0x1000dec2 for n0, je 0x1000e0f3 / jne 0x1000e107 for n1, je 0x1000e335 / jne 0x1000e345 for n2, je 0x1000e39f / jne 0x1000e3af for n3; the type comes from the thunk 0x1000119a = Tile::getType 0x10001f60). Inside the n0 and n1 blocks two more vertices are raised - one of this tile's and one of the neighbour's, reached through Tile::getFaces 0x100153c0 (thunk 0x100010aa) - and two further blocks each raise four vertices, of this tile, of the neighbour, of n2 or n3 and of the diagonal, but only when the diagonal is type 6 too: three tests, on the pointer of THIS tile's n2 / n3, on that tile's type and on the DIAGONAL's type (je 0x1000df26, jne 0x1000df3a, jne 0x1000df51 and their three twins at 0x1000e00b/01f/036, 0x1000e167/17b/192 and 0x1000e24e/262/279). The n2 and n3 blocks at the end raise two vertices each. Twenty-five vertices in all. It then marks the normals of this tile (0x1000e408) and of the eight tiles around it dirty, guarded only by the pointers and NOT by the type (je 0x1000e416, 0x1000e42c/435, 0x1000e44e/457, 0x1000e470, 0x1000e486, 0x1000e49c, 0x1000e4b2/4bb, 0x1000e4d4/4dd), and re-blends the neighbourhood (call 0x1000e4f2 of the thunk 0x1000103c). The two-pointer dirty tests read THIS tile's n2 / n3 and then dereference the OTHER tile's (0x1000e431 against 0x1000e43d), as the vertex walk does (0x1000df22 against 0x1000df46), so a neighbour with a NULL n2 or n3 faults.
