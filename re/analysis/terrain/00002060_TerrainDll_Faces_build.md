## Purpose
Fills a `Faces` record with the eight triangles of a 2 x 2 patch of a terrain tile, together with their texture coordinates. It is __thiscall with three int arguments (`ret 0xc` at 0x10002509) and it writes nothing outside `this`: besides the arguments it reads only the constants 2.0f at 0x1005f028 and 1.0f at 0x1005f024, and the only function it calls is roundHalf 0x10002010 (every `call 0x100012b7` is that thunk). Three signed 32-bit strides are built first, C = arg3 * 3 (0x10002084-0x1000208a), B = arg2 * C * 3 (0x1000208d-0x10002097) and A = arg1 * 3 (0x1000209a-0x100020a0); the count 8 is stored at offset 0 (0x100020a6); then two nested loops over i and j, each fixed at two turns (`cmp ..., 2` and `jge` at 0x100020be/0x100020c2 and 0x100020da/0x100020de), fill TWO records of 0x38 bytes per turn, eight in all. The two records of a turn share the corner j + i * C + B + A at +4 (0x100020ff, 0x1000229b) and differ in their other two vertex indices, which add C and 1 in the two combinations that split a quad into two triangles (0x10002121, 0x10002147, 0x100022c1, 0x100022e4). The six values at +0x10..+0x24 are roundHalf(k/2.0f) and roundHalf(1.0f - k/2.0f) with k one of i, j, i+1, j+1, each converted by `fild` from a frame slot, divided by 2.0f and passed as a float by the `fstp dword ptr [esp]` that pushes it (first pair at 0x1000214b-0x10002169); since i, j, i+1 and j+1 are only ever 0, 1 or 2, those quotients are exactly 0.0f, 0.5f and 1.0f, which is the set roundHalf maps to 0, 2 and 1. Every record also gets 0 at +0x30 (0x1000224d), the float 1.0f written as the literal 0x3f800000 at +0x34 (0x1000225e) and 0 at +0x38 (0x1000226f) - an offset past the 0x38 stride, so that store clears the NEXT record's field at offset 0, which no record writes itself. After the loops twelve DWORDs are copied from one offset of `this` to another (0x10002427-0x100024f3). Its only caller in Terrain.dll is `Terrain::buildArrays` 0x1000a130. Reimplemented in `shim/src/re/c3aw.cpp`; path-1 A/B GREEN: Faces::build (16 vectors, 16 distinct results, 16 changing state, `log/diff/00002060_Terrain_Faces_build.path1.csv`).
