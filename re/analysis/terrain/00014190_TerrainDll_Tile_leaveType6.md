## Purpose
Undoes the raise Tile::enterType6 applied, which is what a tile must do before it stops being type 6 so that the shared corners of the surrounding type-6 tiles come back down. It is the same walk over the same 25 vertices with the same thirteen `== 6` guards (je 0x100141de, jne 0x100141f2 and their twins at 0x10014256/26a/281, 0x1001433b/34f/366, 0x10014423/437, 0x10014497/4ab/4c2, 0x1001457e/592/5a9, 0x10014665/675, 0x100146cf/6df), and 24 of the 25 vertices get `- 20.0f` (fsub dword [0x1005f51c], the first at 0x100141c2). ONE does not: this tile's face 0 slot 0, the first vertex of the n0/n2 corner block, gets `+ 20.0f` (index loaded from [this+0x48] at 0x1001428a, fadd 0x10014296) where the type-6 ENTRY raises the same vertex (fadd 0x1000df66). So that corner is raised twice and never lowered. The dirty walk is unconditional on the type, like the type-6 entry's (0x10014738, then je 0x10014746, 0x1001475c/765, 0x1001477e/787, 0x100147a0, 0x100147b6, 0x100147cc, 0x100147e2/7eb, 0x10014804/80d). Unlike the two entry functions it does NOT re-blend: there is no call of the thunk 0x1000103c anywhere in the body, because Tile::setTypeId runs the entry function of the NEW type right after it and that one re-blends. Reimplemented in `shim/src/re/c3bk.cpp`; path-1 A/B GREEN: Tile::leaveType6 (15 vectors, 15 distinct results, 15 changing state, `log/diff/00014190_Terrain_Tile_leaveType6.path1.csv`); Tile::leaveType6_off (15 vectors, 15 distinct results, 15 changing state, `log/diff/00014190_Terrain_Tile_leaveType6_off.path1.csv`); Tile::leaveType6_b (15 vectors, 15 distinct results, 15 changing state, `log/diff/00014190_Terrain_Tile_leaveType6_b.path1.csv`).
