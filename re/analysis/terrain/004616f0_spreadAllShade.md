## Purpose

It first sums the weight bytes at 0x0053ea24 over 50 rows of 50 (0x004616ff / 0x00461702, the row pointer stopping at 0x0053f3e8 at 0x0046170d), then clears both accumulators with 0x271 dwords of zero each — 0x0082376c at 0x00461724 and 0x00822da0 at 0x00461730, 625 dwords being the whole 2500-byte grid. The spread loop then walks the same 50x50 index (0x00461751 / 0x00461759 / 0x004617bd / 0x004617c7, the row pointer stopping at 0x005a6d3c) and calls spreadShade 0x004615f0 for every non-zero source byte: the positive grid at 0x005a6378 with byte * 20000 / total (the four `lea eax,[eax+eax*4]` plus `shl eax, 5` at 0x00461769..0x00461779 and the call at 0x0046177e) and the negative grid at 0x0056c7e4 with byte * -40000 / total (the negate-times-five at 0x00461796..0x004617a8 and the call at 0x004617b1).
