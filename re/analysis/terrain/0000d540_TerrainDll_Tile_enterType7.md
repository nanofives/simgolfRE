## Role (from the naming pass, not a C3 purpose)

## Purpose
Lowers by 13.0 every mesh vertex that a tile entering terrain type 7 shares with a type-7 neighbour or diagonal, the sunken counterpart of the type-6 entry: the same walk over the same 25 vertices with `fsub dword [0x1005f518]` (= 13.0f, the first at 0x1000d57c) instead of `fadd dword [0x1005f51c]`, and with every guard comparing against 7 (cmp eax, 7 at 0x1000d5a9 and its twelve twins). It differs from Tile::enterType6 in two more ways. It clears the path-type flag [this+0x20a] before anything else (0x1000d566), undoing the 1 Tile::setTypeId had just written there for a tile with a path. And its normals-dirty walk is guarded by the TYPE as well as by the pointers: each of the eight neighbours and diagonals is marked only when Tile::getType says 7 (jne 0x1000db11, 0x1000db43, 0x1000db78, 0x1000dba1, 0x1000dbc7, 0x1000dbed, 0x1000dc1f, 0x1000dc54), where the other three walks of this group mark every tile whose pointer exists. Only this tile itself is marked unconditionally (0x1000daf3). It ends with the re-blend (call 0x1000dc69 of the thunk 0x1000103c).
