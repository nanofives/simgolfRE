## Purpose
clearObjectFootprint (0x0040e400, __cdecl, void, one argument) removes the placed object whose record is at 0x0058bcb8 + index*0x10 (type word +0, tile x word +2, tile y word +4, cost dword +8, the index scaled by `shl edi, 4` at 0x0040e40b) and resets the tiles it covered. A lot (type 5, tested at 0x0040e423) is refunded first: the refund is the cost divided by 50 plus half of rateLot 0x0042ef40 for the lot's tile (0x0040e432..0x0040e45f), it is taken off the cash dword 0x00571fd4 (0x0040e467), shown by pointsPopup 0x0040c890 at the tile centre (0x0040e488), subtracted from the revenue row of the current date (0x00584212 + (0x005a6d3c % 100) * 0x14, 0x0040e48d..0x0040e4a2), and when the cost is non-zero the lot's position records are released by freeRecordAtPos 0x004011b0 (0x0040e4b2 / 0x0040e4c4). The slot is then marked free with 0xffff (0x0040e4cf) and type 0xf additionally raises bit 0x800 of the global flags 0x0059e7b8 (0x0040e4d8). The footprint side is the signed byte at 0x004c26c0 + type*0x14 (0x0040e4f5), widened for types of 6 and above other than 7 by the dword at 0x005a8c38 + type*4 minus one (0x0040e512..0x0040e529); the origin tile's flags word at 0x0053caf0 loses bits 0x021f (mask 0xfde0, 0x0040e505) and every tile of the square footprint loses bits 0x0420 (mask 0xfbdf, 0x0040e574), takes the replacement tile type at 0x005722e8 (0x0040e57e) and has its byte at 0x0056988c zeroed (0x0040e584). The replacement type is 0x11 for a type-0xc object on course kinds 0 and 2 (0x005a34e0, 0x0040e548..0x0040e55a) and otherwise 4 plus one when the current course index 0x0059bf90 is at least 0xc (0x0040e561..0x0040e56e). Finally rebuildHeightfield 0x0042f7a0 is called (0x0040e59b).
