## Purpose
Expands a row of sub-byte pixels into one byte per pixel, in place and backwards, and then rewrites the header to say so. Nothing happens at all unless bit_depth is below 8 (`cmp eax, 8` / `jge 0x100720b4` at 0x10071ea0-0x10071ea3, a signed compare of the zero-extended byte). The width is read into [ebp-8] (0x10071eac) before a three-way test on bit_depth: 1 (`je 0x10071ed9` at 0x10071ebe), 2 (`je 0x10071f6b` at 0x10071ec4) and 4 (`je 0x10071ffd` at 0x10071ece); any other value below 8 jumps straight to the tail (`jmp 0x10072088` at 0x10071ed4), so the header is rewritten although no pixel moves. Each loop starts at sp = row + ((width - 1) >> k) (`shr eax, 3` at 0x10071edf for depth 1) and dp = row + width - 1, with the first shift 7 - ((width + 7) & 7) for depth 1 (0x10071efd), (3 - ((width + 3) & 3)) * 2 for depth 2 (`shl ecx, 1` at 0x10071f99) and (1 - ((width + 1) & 1)) * 4 for depth 4; each iteration stores (*sp >> shift) & mask at *dp and then advances the shift, except that the top shift value resets it to 0 and steps sp back one byte (`cmp dword ptr [ebp-0x14], 7` / `jne` at 0x10071f3a-0x10071f3e for depth 1), the depth-4 else branch storing the constant 4 rather than adding. The counters are compared with the width unsigned (`jae` at 0x10071f22 and its twins), so width 0 moves nothing. The tail, reached by every path that passes the guard, sets bit_depth to 8 (0x1007208b), pixel_depth to channels * 8 truncated to a byte (`shl edx, 3` at 0x10072097, stored at 0x1007209d) and rowbytes to width * channels (`imul` at 0x100720ab, stored at 0x100720b1).
