## Purpose
Reads the thirteen-byte PNG image header and installs it in both the png_struct and the png_info. It refuses a second IHDR (any non-zero mode at png_struct+0x68, 0x100796fb) and any length but thirteen (0x10079712) through png_error 0x10078fe0, sets PNG_HAVE_IHDR (bit 0 of the mode, 0x1007972f), reads the payload with png_crc_read 0x100794b0 (0x10079742) and closes the chunk with png_crc_finish 0x10079510 called with a skip of zero (0x10079750) WITHOUT testing its result, so a CRC that disagrees costs only the warning png_crc_finish itself issues. The payload is width (0x1007975c), height (0x1007976b) and five single bytes the body loads as dwords masked with 0xff (0x10079776 through 0x100797ad), the last of which reaches three bytes past the chunk: bit depth, colour type, compression method, filter method and interlace method. Seven validity tests follow, each ending in png_error: a zero or above-0x7fffffff width or height (0x100797b0-0x100797cc), a bit depth outside {1,2,4,8,16} (0x100797df-0x100797fb), a colour type that is negative, 1, 5 or above 6 (0x1007980e-0x10079824), palette above eight bits or truecolour/alpha below eight (0x10079837-0x10079859), an interlace method of two or more (0x1006986c), and a non-zero compression (0x10079883) or filter (0x1007989a) method. What survives is stored at +0xc8, +0xcc, +0x127, +0x123 and +0x126 (0x100798b7-0x100798e7); the channel count at +0x12a is then derived from the colour-type BYTE just stored rather than from the local, through the seven-entry jump table at 0x100799be (0x100798f2), the pixel depth at +0x129 is the low byte of bit_depth * channels (0x1007994f) and rowbytes at +0xd8 is (width * pixel_depth + 7) >> 3 with an unsigned shift (0x1007996f). Finally png_set_IHDR 0x1007dc70 copies the same nine values into the png_info (0x100799a5), pushed in the order filter, compression, interlace, colour type, bit depth, height, width (0x10079981-0x1007999d). Reimplemented in `shim/src/re/c3ck.cpp`; path-1 A/B GREEN: png_handle_IHDR (16 vectors, 16 distinct results, 16 changing state, `log/diff/000796e0_jgld_png_handle_IHDR.path1.csv`).
