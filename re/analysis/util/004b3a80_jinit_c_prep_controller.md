## Purpose
Creates the pre-processing controller, the stage between the colour converter and the downsampler. Unlike 0x004b0560 the `need_full_buffer` check comes first and does **not** return: ERREXIT 4 (JERR_BAD_BUFFER_MODE) is raised (0x004b3a8c / 0x004b3a91 / 0x004b3a9a) and the controller is built anyway. A 0x40-byte object comes from `mem->alloc_small(cinfo, JPOOL_IMAGE, 0x40)` (0x004b3aa2..0x004b3aa7), is stored at `cinfo+0x14c` (0x004b3aa9) and gets start_pass = 0x004b3b30 (0x004b3aaf). The downsampler at cinfo+0x15c then decides the buffer shape: when its `need_context_rows` (downsample+8, 0x004b3abe / 0x004b3ac3) is set, pre_process_data (prep+4) becomes 0x004b3d40 and create_context_buffer 0x004b3f10 builds the buffers (0x004b3ac6 / 0x004b3acd); otherwise pre_process_data becomes 0x004b3b80 (0x004b3ad8) and each component gets one plain buffer from `mem->alloc_sarray(cinfo, JPOOL_IMAGE, width_in_blocks * max_h_samp_factor * 8 / h_samp_factor, max_v_samp_factor)` in the array at prep+8 (0x004b3b17). That samples-per-row is comp+0x1c times cinfo+0xe0 (0x004b3af3 / 0x004b3afc) shifted left by 3 (0x004b3b03) and divided by comp+8 with a SIGNED division (0x004b3b0a / 0x004b3b0b), i.e. the component's row length measured in the largest component's samples; the row count is cinfo+0xe4 (0x004b3af6 / 0x004b3b06), one row group. `num_components` is re-read at the bottom of every pass (0x004b3b19).
