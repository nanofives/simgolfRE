## Purpose
Carries out the two expansions a tRNS chunk and a sub-eight-bit grey depth ask for: widening packed grey pixels to one byte each and turning a grey or RGB row into a grey+alpha or RGBA row, both backwards in the caller's buffer. `gray` is trans_value->gray when that pointer is not null and 0 otherwise (`cmp dword ptr [ebp+0x10], 0` / `je 0x10077344` at 0x10077330, the 16-bit load at 0x1007733b). For color_type 0 (`test eax, eax` / `jne 0x10077788` at 0x10077328) a bit_depth below 8 (`cmp edx, 8` / `jge 0x100775bf` at 0x1007735b, a signed compare) unpacks one byte per pixel, with a chain of equality tests for 1, 2 and 4 (0x1007736d, 0x10077373, 0x1007737d) and no body for 3, 5, 6 or 7; each unpack body also scales `gray` into the eight-bit range it is about to be compared in, times 0xff, 0x55 or 0x11 (`imul edx, edx, 0xff` at 0x10077395, `imul edx, edx, 0x55` at 0x10077447, `imul eax, eax, 0x11` at 0x10077508). Whether a body ran or not, the tail sets bit_depth 8, pixel_depth 8 and rowbytes = width (0x100775a8-0x100775bc). The tRNS stage then runs only when trans_value is not null (0x100775bf): at bit_depth 8 it writes two bytes per pixel, the alpha 0 when the sample equals `gray` (`cmp edx, eax` / `jne 0x10077632` at 0x1007761d) and 0xff otherwise, and at bit_depth 16 four bytes per pixel comparing the big-endian pair (`shl ecx, 8` at 0x100776c2, the test at 0x100776cf). For color_type 2 with a non-null trans_value (0x10077790 and 0x10077799) the same happens with three comparisons instead of one - red, green and blue must all match for the alpha to be 0 (0x10077800, 0x10077815, 0x10077829 at depth 8 and 0x10077915, 0x10077937, 0x10077958 at depth 16) - writing four or eight bytes per pixel. Each stage ends by rewriting the header to the colour type it produced: 4 with channels 2 and pixel_depth bit_depth * 2 (`shl ecx, 1` at 0x10077764), or 6 with channels 4 and pixel_depth bit_depth * 4 (`shl ecx, 2` at 0x10077a5d), and rowbytes = (width * pixel_depth) >> 3 in both (0x10077777, 0x10077a71). One detail of the depth-4 unpack is not a typo of the transcription: its shift advances with a plain assignment of 4 (0x10077596) and not an increment, so after the first pixel it alternates 4 and 0 like the other two bodies do by addition.
