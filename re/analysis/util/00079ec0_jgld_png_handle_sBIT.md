## Purpose
Reads the significant-bits chunk into the png_struct's png_color_8 at +0x17c and from there into the png_info. Its four-byte scratch is zeroed first, one byte at a time from the top down (0x10079ed8-0x10079eeb), so a payload shorter than four bytes leaves zeros behind rather than frame fill. Without PNG_HAVE_IHDR it calls png_error 0x10078fe0 (0x10079ef4, 0x10079f04); after PNG_HAVE_IDAT the chunk is warned about and consumed (0x10079f17, 0x10079f27, 0x10079f37); with PNG_HAVE_PLTE (bit 1 of the mode, 0x10079f4a) it only WARNS and carries on (0x10079f5a, then 0x10079f62 jumps past the duplicate test), so the duplicate test - the sBIT valid bit 2 at info+8 - is reachable only before the palette (0x10079f64, 0x10079f70, 0x10079f80, 0x10079f90). The expected payload length is three for a palette image (colour type 3 at +0x126, 0x10079fa8) and otherwise the channel count at +0x12a (0x10079fbb), so it is 1, 2, 3 or 4; any other length is warned about and consumed (0x10079fc7, 0x10079fd5, 0x10079fe5). The bytes are read at 0x10079ffe, the chunk closed with a skip of zero at 0x1007a00c and the result tested at 0x1007a014. The five bytes at +0x17c (red, green, blue, gray, alpha) are then filled two different ways, chosen by bit 1 of the colour type (0x1007a028): for a colour image red, green and blue take the first three payload bytes and alpha the fourth (0x1007a035, 0x1007a041, 0x1007a04d, 0x1007a059) and the gray byte at +0x17f is left standing; for a greyscale image the gray byte is written first and red, green and blue all take the SAME first payload byte (0x1007a067-0x1007a08b) with alpha taking the second (0x1007a097). png_set_sBIT 0x1007e070 then copies five bytes of that field, in place in the png_struct, to info+0x44 (0x1007a0a0, 0x1007a0ae). Reimplemented in `shim/src/re/c3ck.cpp`; path-1 A/B GREEN: png_handle_sBIT (14 vectors, 14 distinct results, 14 changing state, `log/diff/00079ec0_jgld_png_handle_sBIT.path1.csv`).
