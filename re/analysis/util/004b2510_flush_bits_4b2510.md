## Purpose
flush_bits(phuff_entropy_ptr entropy) terminates the progressive-Huffman bit stream: it fills the partial byte with one-bits (the inlined emit_bits(entropy, 0x7F, 7), reading put_bits/put_buffer at +0x1c/+0x18 and writing whole bytes to *next_output_byte (+0x10), free_in_buffer at +0x14), then resets put_buffer and put_bits to 0.
