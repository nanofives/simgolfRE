# 0x10051319 cvtdate

## Role (from the naming pass, not a C3 purpose)
Computes a daylight-saving transition date: uses the 365-day/leap (&3) year math, the month day-offset tables at 0x1006891c/0x10068950/0x10068920/0x10068954 and a mod-7 weekday to resolve the Nth-weekday-of-month rule. (`re/names/sound_8.tsv`)
System writeup: `re/analysis/systems/sound_1.md`, `re/analysis/systems/sound_5.md`, `re/analysis/systems/sound_8.md`.

## Purpose
Computes one daylight-saving transition instant and stores it in the six dwords at 0x10068900 to 0x1006891b. The eleven stack arguments are, in frame order, a transition kind at [ebp+8], a date kind at [ebp+0xc], a year at [ebp+0x10], a month at [ebp+0x14], a week number at [ebp+0x18], a weekday at [ebp+0x1c], a day of the month at [ebp+0x20] and hours, minutes, seconds and milliseconds at [ebp+0x24] to [ebp+0x30]. Two 13-dword tables hold the number of days before each month, one for a leap year at 0x1006891c and one for a common year at 0x10068950, each preceded by a filler dword so the month argument is one-based; the year selects between them on its low two bits (0x10051337, 0x100513ba) and the entry one slot further on (0x10068920, 0x10068954) is the last day of that month. With the date kind 1 (0x10051325) the day of the year is the first of the month, that is the table entry plus one at 0x1005134f, moved to the week-th occurrence of the requested weekday: the weekday of that first day comes from the remainder of a signed division by 7 at 0x1005136e of year*365 + day + (year-1)/4 - 25563 (0x10051352, 0x1005135c, 0x10051365), the two arms at 0x10051381 and 0x1005138f differ by exactly one week depending on whether that remainder is past the requested weekday (0x10051377), and a week number of 5 means the last occurrence, so a result past the end of the month loses another seven days (0x10051395, 0x100513ad, 0x100513af). With any other date kind (0x100513b4) the day of the year is simply the table entry plus the day of the month (0x100513cc). The transition kind then picks which triple of globals receives it: kind 1 writes the day of the year to 0x10068904, the year to 0x10068900 and the time of day in milliseconds to 0x10068908 (0x100513d8, 0x100513e4, 0x100513f9), anything else writes the same three to 0x10068914, 0x10068910 and 0x10068918 and first adds the dword at 0x10068874 to the seconds (0x10051412), then carries the millisecond count back into one day: negative adds 86400000 and steps the day of the year back (0x10051429, 0x1005142b, 0x10051430), 86400000 or more subtracts it and steps the day forward (0x1005143f, 0x10051441, 0x10051443), with the corrected day stored again at 0x10051449. Every step is 32-bit wrapping integer arithmetic and the division is `cdq`/`idiv`, whose remainder takes the sign of the dividend.
