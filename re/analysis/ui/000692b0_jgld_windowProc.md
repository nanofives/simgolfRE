## Purpose
The window procedure of every jgld window - Display::createWindow 0x100657e0 stores the address of its incremental-link thunk 0x10001302 at 0x10065836, which is the only reference to it in the four modules. It is a four-way unsigned range split on the message - at or below 0x200 (0x100692ce), below 0x20b (0x10069347), below 0x312 (0x1006937d) and 0x401 (0x100693ae) - with three jump tables inside the first two: msg - 5 indexed through the byte table at 0x10069790 into the six targets at 0x10069778 (0x10069318), msg - 0x101 through the byte table at 0x10069828 into the six at 0x10069810 (0x10069340), and msg - 0x201 straight into the six at 0x1006983a (0x10069376). The message is kept in a local that the subtractions overwrite (0x100692fd, 0x10069322, 0x10069360), so a message below a table's base wraps to a large unsigned value and lands on the default arm; the untouched copy at [ebp+0xc] is what that arm hands to DefWindowProcA (0x10069750, 0x10069758). Fifteen messages are dispatched to a handler each: WM_SIZE 5, WM_ACTIVATE 6, WM_PAINT 0xf, WM_COPYDATA 0x4a, WM_NCHITTEST 0x84, the four key messages 0x100/0x101/0x104/0x105 and WM_CHAR 0x102, WM_MOUSEMOVE 0x200, the six button messages 0x201 to 0x206, WM_MOUSEWHEEL 0x20a, 0x30f, WM_PALETTECHANGED 0x311 and the private 0x401. Most return the `xor eax,eax` that follows the call; WM_NCHITTEST (0x100694ec) and WM_COPYDATA (0x100693eb) have no `xor` and return the handler's own result, 0x30f returns the low sixteen bits of realizePalette 0x100699c0's result (0x10069442), and WM_MOUSEWHEEL (0x10069610) and 0x401 (0x100693da) call their handler AND THEN fall into DefWindowProcA. WM_COMMAND 0x111 is NOT handled: its slot in the 0x101 table is the default one, and the handler named onCommand is the one WM_COPYDATA reaches. Two messages share one body: WM_POWERBROADCAST 0x218 with a zero wParam returns the constant 0x424d5144 (0x10069406), BROADCAST_QUERY_DENY, so the window refuses to let the machine suspend, and any other wParam falls into the WM_SYSCOMMAND body (0x10069404), which swallows 0xf140 and 0xf170 by returning zero (0x1006941d, 0x10069426, 0x1006942a) and sends everything else to DefWindowProcA. Three of the handlers read a module global rather than an argument: the 0x401 arm loads the event sink from VA 0x1012872c twice, once for the vtable and once for ecx (0x100693c2, 0x100693ca), onKey 0x10069c70 reads the display at VA 0x10128420 and onCommand 0x1006a0e0 the command handler at VA 0x10128728. Reimplemented in `shim/src/re/c3ck.cpp`; path-1 A/B GREEN: windowProc (48 vectors, 30 distinct results, 27 changing state, `log/diff/000692b0_jgld_windowProc.path1.csv`).
