## Purpose
membershipReport (0x00406670, __cdecl, void, one argument) builds the membership message for the golfer `g` into the shared text buffer 0x0051a068 and hands it to the message ticker. It empties the buffer (0x00406673), clears bit 31 of the golfer's flag dword at 0x005794c8 + g*0x100 (0x00406687..0x00406696), raises by one the tier byte of the golfer's membership slot (the slot index is the word at 0x0057956e + g*0x100 and the byte is at 0x005849e2 + slot*0x2c, 0x0040669c..0x004066b2) and uses the low three bits of the raised value as the tier (0x004066bb). buildGolferName 0x004676e0 writes the golfer's name into the buffer (0x004066c0); tiers below 3 then append the "applies" clause 0x004c48e8 (0x004066cb) while tiers 3 and above append the upgrade clause 0x004c4924 plus a tier adjective (0x004c48f8 / 0x004c4908 / 0x004c4914 for tiers 3 / 4 / 5, 0x004066ff.. 0x00406705) and the tier name from the pointer table 0x004c2a88 + tier*4 (0x00406742), always followed by 0x004c48cc. Tier 3 then appends the pointer at 0x004c2a94 and the clause 0x004c4884 (0x004067a6), and tiers above 3 the tier name again, the fee clause 0x004c483c, the digit 2 for tier 4 and 5 otherwise (0x00406845, neg/sbb/and 3/add 2 over tier - 4) and 0x004c481c (0x004067dd..0x00406884). Next it looks for a free membership slot by drawing Random::range(0x4b) + 1 until the slot's tier byte is zero, giving up after 1000 draws (0x004068b3..0x004068e3); on success the slot is claimed with 1 (0x004068ff) and the join sentence is appended, its two halves chosen by typeBit7Clear 0x0046c940 for this golfer (0x00406916 and 0x00406980), with the drawn slot's type name at 0x004d6098 + slot*0x230 (0x004069aa..0x004069c1). It then appends the census header 0x004c47a4 and, for each tier from 5 down to 1 and then -1 (skipping 0 at 0x00406a53), counts the membership table entries whose word at +0x2a is set and whose tier byte matches, plus the banned entries (+0x29 == -1) when the tier is -1 (0x00406a66..0x00406a93), appending the tier name, the singular or plural noun (0x004c4780 / 0x004c4784, with 0x004c4790 inserted for tier 2) or the "resigned" word 0x004c4798, the count and 0x004c4778 when the count is non-zero (0x00406a9b..0x00406b83). Finally, when bit 3 of 0x005a5a00 is set (0x00406bd8), a panel showing this golfer (0x004c2e0c == g | 0x100, 0x00406bee) is reset through 0x005a9ccc and 0x00569498, and startMessage 0x0040cb00 is called with (0x80004010, 0, g); a message that is accepted also plays sound 0x38 (0x00406c13 / 0x00406c1f).
