## Purpose
Draws one horizontal dashed run on a 16-bit surface, alternating len1 pixels of c1 with len2 pixels of c2 from a starting phase. The row must be inside the clip rectangle of slot +0xcc (0x1000cfca jl, 0x1000cfe9 jl), the span must not be degenerate (0x1000cff6) and the two colours must not both be -1 (0x1000d001 and 0x1000d007). The Palette is then picked once, from the surface's field +0x7c (0x1000d01c) or from the module global 0x1012873c (0x1000d030), and if there is one the format code of slot +0xe4 element [1] selects one of two arms (0x1000d068 for 0, 0x1000d06e for 1) and anything else ends the call (0x1000d070 to 0x1000d142). The two arms are the same code: in both, c1 is looked up through Palette slot +0x18 and c2 through slot +0x1c (`ff 52 18` at 0x1000d08c and 0x1000d0f2, `ff 52 1c` at 0x1000d0be and 0x1000d124), each only when its own bit 31 is clear (0x1000d080 / 0x1000d0b2 in the first arm, 0x1000d0e6 / 0x1000d118 in the second), so the format code never decides which table a colour comes from. Both colours are then masked to 16 bits (0x1000d14a, 0x1000d155), which is why a lone -1 survives as 0xffff and is drawn like any other colour. x1 > x2 swaps the two ends, the two run lengths and the two colours and turns the phase into len1 + len2 - phase (0x1000d164 guarding 0x1000d166..0x1000d1c0); the span is dropped when it lies right of the rectangle (0x1000d1e0) or left of it (0x1000d1fe), x1 is raised to left (0x1000d221), x2 lowered to right - 1 (0x1000d25c) and the pixel address of slot +0x1c (0x1000d290) must be non-NULL (0x1000d2a1). The phase is reduced modulo len1 + len2 by the signed `idiv` at 0x1000d2b2 and turned into a signed cursor: at or past len1 it becomes len2 - (phase - len1), below len1 it becomes -(len1 - phase) (0x1000d2bd and the two arms at 0x1000d2bf and 0x1000d2d3), and len1 is negated (0x1000d2e7). A non-negative cursor is inside a c2 run and a negative one inside a c1 run; each pixel moves it one step toward zero and a cursor that reaches zero is reloaded with the negated len1 or with len2. The loop that does this parks its pixel count in the module global 0x10128450 (0x1000d380) so that ebp can be the counter (0x1000d38b), writes c2 at 0x1000d395 and c1 at 0x1000d39f and steps two bytes per pixel (0x1000d3a7). Slot +0x24 with 1 closes (0x1000d3bc).
