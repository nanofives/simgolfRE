## Purpose
Copies a w x h block of 8-bit pixels out of `this` at (sx, sy) into the surface given as the first argument at (dx, dy), clipped to that surface's clip rectangle, choosing between five copy loops so that a copy whose source and destination are the SAME surface still reads every byte before it is overwritten. It returns 7 when `this` has no pixel base ([this+0x4c0] tested directly at 0x10013c76), 16 for a null destination (0x10013c89), 0 when the block is empty after clipping (0x10013cf1, 0x10013d14, 0x10013ddf) and 3 when either pixel address comes back NULL (0x10013e45, 0x10013e74 and the matching pairs in the other bodies), otherwise 0 after releasing both surfaces through vtable slot +0x24 (0x10014348, 0x1001435e). The source point is pulled into the first quadrant first: a negative sx adds itself to w, is subtracted from dx and becomes 0 (0x10013c99), a negative sy does the same to h and dy (0x10013cb8), and w and h are then cut to what is left of `this`'s width and height (slots +0xd8 and +0xdc, 0x10013d3a and 0x10013d78). rectFromXYWH 0x10008360 builds the destination rectangle (0x10013dad) and intersect 0x10008590 clips it to the destination's clip rectangle (0x10013dd5); what the clip trimmed off the left and the top is added back to sx and sy (0x10013de8, 0x10013df6) and w and h become the intersection's (0x10013e04, 0x10013e0d). `cmp` of the two surface pointers at 0x10013e19 then picks the body: different surfaces, or the same surface with dx < sx and dy < sy (0x10013ef1, 0x10013f03), copy each row forward and step both cursors by pitch - w; the same surface with dx < sx and dy >= sy starts on the last row and steps back by pitch + w (0x10013fd8); the same surface with dx >= sx copies each row BACKWARD with the direction flag set (`std` at 0x100141ad and 0x100142fb), top to bottom (0x100140b5) or bottom to top (0x100141f3). Every body copies a row as `rep movsd` of cols >> 2 dwords followed by `rep movsb` of cols & 3 bytes, the remainder parked in the module global 0x101284b4 (0x10013ece and four more sites) and re-read once per row; both row strides are read as the FIELD [surface+0x40] (0x10013e99, 0x10013ea2), not through slot +0xe0. The two backward bodies are only correct when cols < 4 or cols is a multiple of 4: they start the cursor at cols - 4 from the left of the row (0x10014121, 0x10014268) and the descending `rep movsb` therefore continues four bytes BELOW where the descending `rep movsd` stopped, writing the (cols & 3) bytes that end at rc.left - 3 instead of the leftmost (cols & 3) bytes of the rectangle. The A/B drives that case with cols 10 on a surface where rc.left is 8, and both arms write the same bytes outside the rectangle.
