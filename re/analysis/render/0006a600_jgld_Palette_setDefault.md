## Role (from the naming pass, not a C3 purpose)
fills the 256 palette entries: first/last 10 from the default system-colour tables (0x1011dab0,0x1011dad8), the rest as a greyscale ramp, then calls update (`re/names/jgld_1.tsv`)

## Purpose
Fills all 256 entries of a Palette from nothing but constants and the entry index - its output does not depend on its input at all - and then applies them. It first calls slot 2 of its own vtable (0x1006a627), which in the Palette vtable VA 0x1011db10 is Palette::release 0x1006a380 (through the incremental-link thunk 0x10001451): that zeroes the generation id at +0x810 and the applied id at +0x814, so the Palette::apply 0x1006a4a0 at the end always has work to do. Entries 0 to 9 take the ten RGBQUADs at VA 0x1011dab0 and entries 0xf6 to 0xff the ten at VA 0x1011dad8, each read backwards - red from +2, green from +1, blue from +0 (0x1006a656, 0x1006a66a, 0x1006a67e; 0x1006a69d, 0x1006a6b4, 0x1006a6cb) - because an RGBQUAD stores blue first and a PALETTEENTRY red first, and both blocks get flags 4 (0x1006a68f, 0x1006a6df). Entries 10 to 0xf5 are a greyscale ramp whose three components are all the low byte of the index (0x1006a70d, 0x1006a71a, 0x1006a727) with flags 5 (0x1006a734). Finally entries 8, 9, 0xf6 and 0xf7 are overwritten as system-palette aliases with green and blue zero and flags 2, their red byte being the index 8 or 9 for the low pair (0x1006a75f) and the index MINUS ONE, 0xf5 or 0xf6, for the high pair (0x1006a792). Palette::update 0x1006a3d0 (0x1006a7cb) then draws a new generation id and derives the RGB555 table at +0x40c and the RGB565 table at +0x60c from the entries. The return value is the `xor eax,eax` of 0x1006a7d0.
