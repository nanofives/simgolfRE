Ends given the wrong way round swap not only the ends but also the two run lengths and the two colours, and the phase becomes len1 + len2 - phase (0x10011564 guards 0x10011566..0x100115c0); `x1` is then raised to `left` (0x10011621) and `x2` lowered to `right - 1` (0x1001165c). The phase is reduced modulo len1 + len2 by the signed division at 0x100116b2 and turned into a signed cursor — a phase at or past `len1` becomes len2 - (phase - len1), a phase below it becomes -(len1 - phase) (0x100116bd and the two arms at 0x100116bf and 0x100116d3) — after which `len1` is negated (0x100116e7). A non-negative cursor is inside a `c2` run and a negative one inside a `c1` run; each pixel moves the cursor one step toward zero and, on reaching zero, reloads it with the negated len1 or with len2 (0x10011713..0x10011718, 0x1001171a..0x1001171d). Which of the three inline loops runs is decided after the swap: c1 == -1 selects the one that writes only in the non-negative half (0x100116f0 guards 0x100116f2), c2 == -1 the one that writes only in the negative half (0x1001172b guards 0x1001172d), and otherwise both halves write (0x10011762).
