## Purpose
Over the clipped rectangle (x, y, w, h) of this 16-bit surface, replaces every pixel that holds the first colour with the second and leaves every other pixel alone: a two-colour substitution, not a fill. It is the 16-bit twin of Surface::fill8 0xf880 (batch c3av), with the same four calls in the same order and two bytes per pixel instead of one. It returns 0 for an empty intersection (0x1000b043), 7 when the pixel address is NULL (0x1000b06f, with nothing released) and 0 after releasing the surface once through slot +0x24 with 1 (0x1000b0db). rectFromXYWH 0x10008360 builds the rectangle (0x1000b011) and intersect 0x10008590 clips it (0x1000b039) with the arguments (&r, clipRect(), &r) — the clip rectangle of slot +0xcc is the SECOND argument here, where blitT_8to16 0x12db0 passes it as the third. The pixel address comes from slot +0x1c at the intersection's top-left corner (0x1000b05e); the loop's width and height are then taken from the intersection by rectWidth 0x10009120 and rectHeight 0x10009160 (0x1000b07c, 0x1000b08b) and written back over the w and h ARGUMENT slots (0x1000b084, 0x1000b093); and the row skip is (pitch - w) * 2 bytes from the FIELD [this+0x40] (0x1000b099), not through slot +0xe0. Both colours are read as 16-bit words from their argument slots (0x1000b0a6, 0x1000b0aa), so the upper half of either argument is ignored. The loop compares each destination word with the first colour (0x1000b0ba) and stores the second only where they are equal (0x1000b0bd), steps two bytes per pixel (0x1000b0c2), re-reads the pixel count from the w slot at the top of every row (0x1000b0b7) and adds the skip after each (0x1000b0c8). Reimplemented in `shim/src/re/c3bh.cpp`; path-1 A/B GREEN: Surface::fill16 (17 vectors, 14 distinct results, 12 changing state, `log/diff/0000afe0_jgld_Surface_fill16.path1.csv`).
