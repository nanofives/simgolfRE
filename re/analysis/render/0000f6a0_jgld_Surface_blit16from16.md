## Purpose
Recolours a rectangle of `this` through a caller-supplied lookup table, taking the colour from the surface given as the second argument wherever `this` already holds the magic word 0x7c1f and from `this` itself everywhere else, which makes it the shaded overlay of one 16-bit surface onto another. The shade is the only floating point: the alpha argument is loaded as an integer (0x1000f6bd), divided by the float 100.0f at VA 0x1011d34c (0x1000f6c0), multiplied by the float 15.0f at VA 0x1011d348 (0x1000f6c6) and truncated by the CRT `__ftol` at 0x1007f12c (0x1000f6cc) with no intermediate store, and the index base is (15 - that) shifted left 15 (0x1000f6d1..0x1000f6d8), so an alpha of 0 selects the darkest of sixteen 0x8000-word bands and an alpha of 100 the undarkened one. The rectangle argument is intersected IN PLACE with `this`'s bounds rectangle (slot +0xd4 at 0x1000f6ee) by USER32's IntersectRect through jgld's import slot 0x1012c5e4 (0x1000f706) and an empty intersection returns 3 (0x1000f715); the function returns 0 on every other path (0x1000f801) and releases nothing. Both pixel addresses are taken from slot +0x1c at the intersection's top-left corner, `this`'s at 0x1000f738 and the source's at 0x1000f75c, and NEITHER is tested for NULL, so a surface without pixels would fault. The row skip is (pitch() - w) * 2 from `this`'s slot +0xe0 (0x1000f791..0x1000f7a3) and is added to BOTH cursors (0x1000f7f0, 0x1000f7f3), so a source whose stride differs from `this`'s walks off its own rows. The two loop counters live in the two halves of one register, w in the high half and h in the low half (0x1000f7ae..0x1000f7b4), and are counted down sixteen bits at a time (`dec cx` 0x1000f7eb, `dec dx` 0x1000f7fa), so a zero width or height would run 65536 times; the intersection makes both at least 1. Per pixel the shade is OR'd with a 16-bit colour in its low half only (0x1000f7cd, 0x1000f7da) and the table entry at that index is written over the destination word (0x1000f7d0 / 0x1000f7d5 and 0x1000f7dd / 0x1000f7e2); which colour is OR'd in is the only use of the second surface (0x1000f7c6 cmp, 0x1000f7cb jne). Reimplemented in `shim/src/re/c3bc.cpp`; path-1 A/B GREEN: Surface::blit16from16 (22 vectors, 21 distinct results, 22 changing state, `log/diff/0000f6a0_jgld_Surface_blit16from16.path1.csv`).
