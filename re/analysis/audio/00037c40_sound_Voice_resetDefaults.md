## Role (from the naming pass, not a C3 purpose)
fastcall(voice): clears the envelope/position fields and sets playback defaults - unity rate +0x50/0x68/0x78=0x10000 and gain 1.0 (+0x5c/0x64/0x74/0x84 = 0x3ff00000, the high dword of 1.0). (`re/names/sound_4.tsv`)
System writeup: `re/analysis/systems/sound_4.md`.

## Purpose
Resets a voice record to its defaults: its fields to 0, 0x10000 or the high word of 1.0 (0x10037c5d-0x10037cb8, 0x10037d1d-0x10037d6e) and returns this (0x10037d78). With g_soundEngine 0x100b4a20 set it also derives the rate ratio from the engine's output rate R (vtable +0x40, 0x10037cca): when 1000.0 (0x1005b280) is above R (`fcomp` 0x10037cdf), [+0xf0] = [+0xf8] = 1.0 / (1000 / R) (0x10037ce8-0x10037cf8), otherwise 1.0; the __ftol result it stores at +0xec (0x10037cfb) is overwritten with 0 at 0x10037d47. Runs under the caller's word: every engine key differs between 0x027f and 0x0f7f (log/c3/alt/c3fh_cwpair.txt).
