## Purpose
Sets a channel's base rate; MultiSound::setVolume 0x1003a0d0 calls it (0x1003a115, shim/src/re/c3cx.cpp). __thiscall (rate), `ret 4`. The rate is clamped to 0..0x10000 (signed compares 0x100351cc / 0x100351d7) and stored at +0x208; rate * 2^-16 (0x1005c5e8) goes to +0x210 and, unless +0x58 bit 3, to +0x1e0 with the rate at +0x1d8 (0x100351e2-0x10035206). It re-applies the pan through Channel_setPanGains (thunk 0x10001a7d, 0x10035219) and, when +0x58 has bit 0 and not 0x10000000 (0x10035221-0x1003522e), copies the three gains into every voice record not flagged with bit 3 of [s-0x50] and recomputes that voice's word and gain against the engine rate exactly as Channel_recomputeVoiceGains does, with the rate +0x1c0 (0x1003524c-0x100352d0). eax at exit is the last value loaded: the flag word, a voice word or __ftol's result, and MultiSound::setVolume returns it (shim/src/re/c3cx.cpp).
