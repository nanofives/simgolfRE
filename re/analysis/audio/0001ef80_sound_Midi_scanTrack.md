## Purpose
Walks a MIDI track to its end-of-track meta event and returns the sum of its delta times, which it also stores at [this+0x54] (0x1001f049). The track bytes come from [this+0x3c] (0x1001ef85) and the running status starts at 0 (0x1001ef88). Each round decodes a variable-length delta time inline, seven bits per byte, most significant first (`shl esi, 7` / `or esi, ecx` at 0x1001efac-0x1001efaf, continuing while bit 7 is set, `jne 0x1001efa2` at 0x1001efb5), adds it to the total (0x1001efc1) and looks at the byte that follows: a byte with bit 7 set becomes the running status, kept whole when its high nibble is 0xf0 and reduced to its high nibble otherwise (`je 0x1001efe4` at 0x1001efe0), and is stepped over (0x1001efe4); a byte without bit 7 leaves the previous status in place, which is how running status is handled. A status outside 0x80..0xff makes the function return 0 without writing anything (`ja 0x1001f051` at 0x1001efeb, UNSIGNED). Otherwise the byte table at 0x1001f06c, read through the jump table at 0x1001f05c (0x1001efef-0x1001eff5), gives the event length: 0x80 and 0x90 skip two data bytes (0x1001f02f), 0xc0 and 0xe0 skip one (0x1001f02c), 0xff is the meta event (0x1001effc) and every other status, including 0xa0, 0xd0 and the system messages 0xf0..0xfe, returns 0 (0x1001f051). The meta case marks the track finished when the meta type is 0x2f (0x1001f000-0x1001f002), steps over the type byte (0x1001f00a) and skips the payload by decoding a second variable-length quantity and advancing past both it and its own byte count (0x1001f00f-0x1001f028). The walk ends on the finished flag (`je 0x1001ef9c` at 0x1001f038).
