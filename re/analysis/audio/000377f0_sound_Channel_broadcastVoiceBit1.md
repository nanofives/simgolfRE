Bit 0 of the channel flag dword [ecx+0x58] (read at 0x100377f0, `test al, 1` at 0x100377f3, `je 0x100377f5`) picks the body. With bit 0 set, bit 0 of the argument is moved to bit 1 (`and dl, 1` at 0x100377fc, `shl dl, 1` at 0x10037805) and written into the flag byte of all 0x10 voice records (`mov esi, 0x10` at 0x10037807; the body at 0x1003780c to 0x1003781f reads [eax], advances eax by the 0x110 record stride, clears bit 1 with `and cl, 0xfd` at 0x10037813, ors the new bit in at 0x10037816 and stores back to [eax-0x110]), starting at [ecx+0x2d4] (`lea eax, [ecx+0x2d4]` at 0x100377ff); the channel flag dword itself is left alone.
