## Purpose
Rewinds every track of a sequence and clears the sequence's own run flags. __thiscall with no stack argument (`ret` at 0x1001e20a), always returning 0 (`xor eax, eax` at 0x1001e207). The track list's head is read from [ecx+0x19c] (0x1001e1c3) and the walk position is kept in the sequence's cursor field [ecx+0x1a4] (written at 0x1001e1cb before the first test and at 0x1001e1f0 inside the loop); each node holds its successor at +4 (0x1001e1eb) and its track record at +8 (0x1001e1d3 and 0x1001e1f8). Every track record is passed to Seq_rewindTracks (VA 0x1001e8e0, called at 0x1001e1dc through the incremental-link thunk) with the record in ecx. The walk ends on a null head (`je 0x1001e1d1`), a null track record on the first node (`je 0x1001e1d8`), a null cursor when it is re-read from [ecx+0x1a4] after the call (`je 0x1001e1e9`), a null successor (`je 0x1001e1f6`) or a null track record inside the loop (`jne 0x1001e1fd` closes it). Re-reading the cursor from memory after each callee call rather than keeping it in a register means the callee can stop the walk by zeroing [ecx+0x1a4]. Afterwards bits 2, 3, 4 and 7 of the low byte of [ecx+0x4c] are cleared (`and al, 0x63` at 0x1001e202, stored at 0x1001e204), which leaves bits 0, 1, 5, 6 of that byte and all of bits 8..31 unchanged, and that store happens on every path including the null-head one.
