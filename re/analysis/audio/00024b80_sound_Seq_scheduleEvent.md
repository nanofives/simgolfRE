## Role (from the naming pass, not a C3 purpose)
Computes an event's next trigger tick into event+0x50 from param_2 and the quantization flags in param_3 (bit0 round up to the current event length, bit2 round up to the loop size +0x1bc, bit4 absolute, else 0); resolves the target event by index param_4 via Seq_listGetAt. (`re/names/sound_3.tsv`)

## Purpose
Sets the tick at which a sequencer track is next serviced, choosing the rule from a flag byte. The function leaves no defined value in eax on any path (the epilogue at 0x10024c0f pops and returns without writing it), so it is void. The period starts as the track record's dword at +0x54 (0x10024b8f); a track index above zero (`test eax, eax` / `jle 0x10024bad` at 0x10024b8d, SIGNED) looks that track up with Seq_listGetAt 0x1001d780 on the list at this+0x60 (0x10024b9d) and, when the lookup succeeded (`jne 0x10024bad` at 0x10024ba4), takes the period from the record it found instead (0x10024baa). A zero period does nothing at all (`je 0x10024c0f` at 0x10024baf), which also keeps the `div esi` at 0x10024bc3 from dividing by zero. The flag byte then picks one of four ways to write the record's dword at +0x50: bit 0 (`je 0x10024bce` at 0x10024bb9) gives `tick + (period - tick % period)`, the next multiple of the period at or after the tick (0x10024bc1-0x10024bc9, UNSIGNED); bit 2 (`je 0x10024bea` at 0x10024bd1) gives `(tick / step + 1) * step` with the step the sequencer's dword at +0x1bc, i.e. the next multiple of the sequencer's own quantum STRICTLY after the tick (0x10024bd3-0x10024be5); bit 4 (`je 0x10024bf8` at 0x10024bed) gives the tick itself (0x10024bf3); and no bit at all gives zero (0x10024bf8). Bit 5 finally advances the track to that point by handing it to Midi_seekTrackToTick 0x10024920 with a tick limit of 0 (`test bl, 0x20` / `je 0x10024c0f` at 0x10024bff, call at 0x10024c0a). Reimplemented in `shim/src/re/c3bi.cpp`; path-1 A/B GREEN: Seq_scheduleEvent (14 vectors, 12 distinct results, 11 changing state, `log/diff/00024b80_sound_Seq_scheduleEvent.path1.csv`).
