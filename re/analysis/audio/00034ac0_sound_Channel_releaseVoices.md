## Purpose
Marks a multi-sound channel's voices as released. Three flag bits of the channel word at [ecx+0x58] gate the work (0x10034ac0): bit 6 clear or bit 4 set means the channel is not in a releasable state and the function returns 0x15 without a write (0x10034ac3, 0x10034acb, 0x10034b4b), while bit 0 clear or bit 28 set means there is nothing to sweep and it only sets bit 3 of that word (0x10034acf, 0x10034ad3, 0x10034b43). Otherwise it walks the 0x10 voice records of 0x110 bytes from [ecx+0x2d4] (0x10034ae1, 0x10034b35, 0x10034b3b) while bits 12..27 of the flag word are not all clear (0x10034af0) and inspects each voice's two doubles against the qword 0.0 at 0x1005b498 (0x10034afc, 0x10034b0c): a voice whose doubles are both non-zero and ordered is only flagged as fading (bit 3 of its flag byte, 0x10034b19), while a voice with a zero or unordered double is released outright, its dword at +0x48 cleared, bit 2 of its flag byte set and bit 0 of the byte eight before it cleared (0x10034b20-0x10034b30). Returns 0 (0x10034b3f). Its caller is MultiSound_release 0x10039c40.
