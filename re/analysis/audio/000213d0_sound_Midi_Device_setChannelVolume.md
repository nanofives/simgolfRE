## Purpose
Handles the two MIDI controllers that carry a channel's mix position, and pushes a volume change straight through to the voices that are playing on that channel. It always returns 2 (0x10021400, 0x100214ff). Controller 0xa stores the value in the device's dword at `this + 0x4c * channel + 0x260` (`lea edx, [eax+eax*8+0x48]` / `lea eax, [eax+edx*2+8]` at 0x100213f3-0x100213fb, i.e. 19 * channel + 0x98 dwords, so a channel block is 0x4c bytes) and does nothing else, and a controller that is neither 0xa nor 7 does nothing at all (`cmp al, 0xa` / `jne 0x100213fe` at 0x100213e6). Controller 7 (`cmp al, 7` / `je 0x1002140c` at 0x100213de) stores the value in the dword at `this + 0x4c * channel + 0x254` (0x10021415-0x1002141b) and then, unless bit 0 of the state object's byte at +0x38 is set (`jne 0x100213fe` at 0x1002142a) or its dword at +0x48 names another channel (`jne 0x100213fe` at 0x1002142f), walks the 0x80 group pointers from the state object's +0x254 DOWNWARDS, four bytes at a time (0x10021431, 0x1002143c, 0x100214ea-0x100214f6). Null pointers are skipped (`je 0x100214e2` at 0x1002144c). A group with bit 0 of its dword at +0xc8 clear (`je 0x100214d3` at 0x10021459) gets the raw value through its own vtable slot +0x40 (`call dword [eax+0x40]` at 0x100214db, one argument, the object in ecx). With that bit set, the sixteen voice slots at group+0x2a8, stride 0x110, are walked (0x10021464, `add ebx, 0x110` at 0x100214c2, `jl 0x1002146a` at 0x100214cb) and bits 12..27 of the group's +0xc8 select which slot becomes the current one (`shr eax, 0xc` / `and eax, 0xffff` / `test ebp, eax` at 0x1002146a-0x10021478); that selection is STICKY, so a slot stays current for every later round until another bit selects a new one. Whenever a current slot exists and bit 3 of its byte at +0x9c is clear (0x10021485, 0x1002148e), Voice_setLevel 0x100383b0 is called on it with `((slot[+0xe8] * value) >> 7) * this[+0x730] >> 7`, both shifts UNSIGNED (0x10021499-0x100214b2), i.e. the voice's own level scaled by the controller value and by the device's master scale, each in 1/128 steps.
