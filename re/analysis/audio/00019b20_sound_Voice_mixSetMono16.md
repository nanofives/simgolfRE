It then resets the FPU and loads its own control word 0x0f7f (`mov [ebp-0x24], 0xf7f` 0x10019ca8, `fninit` 0x10019cbf, `fldcw` 0x10019cc1: 64-bit precision, round toward zero) and keeps gains, step and position on the register stack for the whole loop: position += step (0x10019cd0), integer part by `fist` (toward zero), the fraction by `fisub` (0x10019cde), linear interpolation between the two samples (0x10019cf2-0x10019cf6), times each gain (0x10019cfe / 0x10019d02), stored as two doubles per frame (0x10019d06 / 0x10019d08). At the window end it loops to the loop start (with the +0x5c / +0x20 wrap handling, 0x10019d26-0x10019d53) or stops; the sample past the end is the loop start's or the word at [ebp+0x16], set to 0 (0x10019d55). It never restores the control word and returns with 4 (count exit) or 5 (end exit) values on the register stack, which the reimplementation pushes back in the same order (c3fh_ab.py compares cw, status word, tag word and every register: equal).
