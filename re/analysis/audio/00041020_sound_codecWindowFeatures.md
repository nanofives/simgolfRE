## Purpose

The frame range and the rebase descriptor are decremented by one dword (0x10041041, 0x1004104a); the second sample array is then rebased by the descriptor's dword at +0xc and the first by its dword at +4 (0x10041053-0x1004107b), so both are one-based in the frame's own numbering. The window length is `range[8] - range[4] + 1` (0x100410cf-0x100410de) and the sub-frame runs from `range[4] + ((subframe - 1) * window) / 2 + 1` for half a window (0x100410e1-0x1004110e, both divisions SIGNED), so consecutive sub-frames half-overlap. The running sign starts as floatCopySign of the float 1.0f at 0x10064e40 carrying the sign of `first[start-1] - dither` (0x1004111a-0x10041134). Each sample adds |second[i]| (0x10041186), |first[i]| (0x100411bb), |first[i] - first[i-1]| (0x100411f7, the difference kept unrounded by `fst dword` at 0x100411d7), first[i] squared (0x10041212), first[i] * first[i-1] into the caller's slot (0x1004122e), second[i] squared (0x10041247), second[i-lag] squared (0x10041264), second[i+lag] squared (0x10041281), second[i] * second[i+lag] (0x1004129e) and second[i] * second[i-lag] (0x100412bb). The dithered sample `first[i] + dither` goes through floatCopySign (0x100412db) and a sign that differs from the running one (`fcompp` at 0x100412e6, `test ah, 0x40` / `jne 0x10041304` at 0x100412ea) increments the crossing count and flips the running sign (0x100412f4, 0x100412ff); the dither itself is negated every sample (0x10041309), which is what makes it a dither rather than an offset.
