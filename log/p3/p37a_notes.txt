   MODEL (tick.cpp "trace slices", TraceInput; time_model.md section 7):
   - A DISPATCH is one pump call's root-handler calls (port/test/trace_commands.py's batches: HANDLER records with no clock
     read between them but the handlers' own). Its pump CLASS comes from the last clock site before it: the frame head or
     jgld's Palette::update QPC = a pump of the frame body, anything else = a modal loop. WHICH body pump: the frame's cursor
     reads before it. The frame body reads the cursor in P6's status bar, after P6's pumpInput483bd0 and in H1 after its
     pumpMessages (course_season/03 frame 1: CURSOR, KBSTATE, dispatch, CURSOR, TAKE, TAKE, CURSOR): 4,329 of the corpus's
     dispatches come in P6's pumpInput483bd0, not in H1 (time_model.md's "H1" counted the clock site only). A pump takes the
     frame's next dispatch of its class once the port has made as many cursor reads in the frame as the original had before
     it (a Blocking loop without cursor reads, pumpAndRedraw's, at its second refused call); a Drain pump takes none (a
     message arrives between two pump calls only inside a clock call, which ends a batch: U-0941). Over the 109 PASSing
     segments: 4,192 dispatches delivered, 0 left, 62 at another cursor count, cursor reads 0 over / 1,909 under (the port's
     modal loops read the cursor less often than the original's), GetAsyncKeyState reads 0 over / 0 under.
   - GetAsyncKeyState outside the root handlers (P6 0x10, H2 0x10 / 0x11): the frame's KEYSTATE records in order, per key.
     The press handlers' own Ctrl / Shift and the deadline clocks of handlers 2 / 3 / 7 go into each Command.
   - DELIVER w32 (the harness's memory writes): applied before the first dispatch that follows them, at H1's frame wait
     (TkBase::wait_point, up to the first dispatch after H1's g_frameMs read), or at the frame's end; one made inside the
     frame-head clock call applies before the frame body. The other DELIVER kinds are visible through the records they cause.
   - queued_messages (start.json, 2 segments): the slice's HANDLER records already hold their dispatch; both PASS.
   - END CHECKPOINT inside a modal wait (pumpAndRedraw's or a screen loop's clock site): the last frame runs until its
     Blocking pump has no recorded input left (NeedInput) and that state is compared; 5 PASSing segments end so.
