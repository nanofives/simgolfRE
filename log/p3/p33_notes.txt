Phase 3.3 (+ 3.6) golfers, walkers, animals and habitats, notes (2026-10-19, child "SimGolf 3.3/3.6 golfers port")

Scope
- re/port/simcore_order.tsv rows with phase3_child 3.3 (13) and 3.6 (8): 21 functions. Ported 19 in module `golfers`
  (port/src/sim/golfers.cpp + golfers_glue.inc, golfers_world.inc, golfers_walkers.inc, golfers_pro.inc;
  port/include/portsim/sim/golfers.h). Not ported: loadGolferRoster (U-0864), skillPointDialog (U-0865).
- composeGolferSprite is ported for its simulation effect only (the look-index write-back into the type record); the layers
  are drawing (U-0861).

Method
- Written from the analysis notes, the hook sources (shim/src/re/*.cpp, reading reference for per-instruction semantics)
  and the vector corpus; every original address in a hook was mapped to a GameState member + field with
  port/include/gamestate_meta.inc (no literal in port code). Unbounded indices go through Ptr arithmetic (spawnWalker's
  free-slot search, model_defects#11; golfer index -1 reads; placeHabitat's slot -1 link write).
- Peers are called through the registry by their hooks.csv names (MissingResource when absent): updateMembership (3.2),
  holeQuadrants / loadThemeFile (3.1b), pathStep / freeAtTile / cornerHeights / heightBlend / clearObjectFootprint (3.1a),
  thoughtFlag / emitGolferThought (3.5). C++ calls only into the committed core (util, tiles).
- The view / sim bridge (parent's decision after the first report): module `bridge` (port/src/sim/bridge.cpp,
  portsim/sim/bridge.h, port/test/test_bridge.py) registers worldToScreen, playSoundAt (simulation part), queueMessage and
  pointsPopup under their hooks.csv names; golfers calls them directly (the earlier local copies in golfers_glue.inc are gone),
  3.4 / 3.5 by name. golfers.h includes bridge.h so shots.cpp, which already used world_to_screen through golfers.h, builds
  unchanged. playSound (audio), cellAt and enqueueSprite have no simulation effect and are dropped without a registered backend.
  playSoundAt draws g_rng only when worldToScreen puts the point on screen: the sim RNG depends on the camera (U-0860).
- updateWalkers was verified before 3.1a registered pathStep with a scratch transcription of pathStep in a private build
  (scratchpad only, never committed): pathStep 281/281, updateWalkers 92/92. With 3.1a's pathStep: 92/92.
- A private build (scratchpad copy of port/ with only compiling modules, SG_PORTSIM_DLL) was used while siblings' files were
  mid-edit; the final runs are on the tree build.

Defects reproduced (model_defects.md section 5, entries 40..45, no compatibility switch: each is a fault or a loop the
original can never leave, reported as SG_E_DIVIDE / SG_E_NO_RETURN like decaySum in 3.0, or a dead computation):
- 40 placeHabitat's cluster loop never advances its slot (runs until slot + 1 is taken; NoReturn when it cannot be).
- 41 spawnObject claims the record before its cell search, which never ends with no free cell (NoReturn).
- 42 loadGolfPro divides by 4 - difficulty and by (5 * level + 20) * 2; no filled pro record: NoReturn.
- 43 placeAnimal's idiv by the frame count.
- 44 resetGolferState works on one hole record, thresholds twice.
- 45 addGolferToTable's discarded distance comparison: only the RNG draws survive.
Also reproduced: model_defects#8 (placeHabitat reads the byte after slot 127), #11 (spawnWalker unbounded), #16 (updateWalkers
reads one byte of cols[400]), updateWalkers' x subtracted in the y expression (CLAUDE.md simulation plan).
